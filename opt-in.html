How Text Message Opt-In Works

Encore Marketing System, last updated October 5, 2026

Encore Marketing System provides loyalty, review-reply, and messaging tools for small retail stores. This page shows exactly how a person opts in to receive text messages from Encore. Opting in is voluntary and is never required to get a consultation or to buy anything.

Who receives our text messages

Our text messages go to business owners (our merchant customers) who asked about our services or became customers. They are service messages: consultation confirmations and follow-ups, onboarding and account notices, and reminders the owner asks us to set. We do not send promotional messages to consumers under this program.

How a business owner opts in

On encoremarketingsystem.com, the owner fills in the free consultation request form: store name, name, mobile number, and a message. The form has a separate, optional, unchecked text message box, shown below with its exact wording. The box links to our Privacy Policy and Terms. The date, time, and wording of each consent are stored with the request.

Confirmation, Help, and Opt-Out

Opt-in confirmation: Encore Marketing System: You're subscribed to consultation and service text updates. Msg freq varies. Msg and data rates may apply. Reply HELP for help, STOP to cancel.
HELP reply: Encore Marketing System: For help email tmartbakersfield@gmail.com or call 661-373-7950. Msg and data rates may apply. Reply STOP to cancel.
STOP reply: Encore Marketing System: You have been unsubscribed and will receive no more messages. Reply START to rejoin.

Sample Messages

Encore Marketing System: We received your consultation request, [Name]. We will contact you soon. Reply STOP to opt out.
Encore Marketing System: Your pilot setup visit is set for Oct 12 at 10:00 AM. Reply STOP to opt out.
Encore Marketing System: Reminder you asked us to set: supplier order due tomorrow at 9:00 AM. Reply STOP to opt out.

Messages are sent only to owners who opted in, and any number that replies STOP is removed from sending right away.

Store customer messaging (separate programs)

Individual stores may later offer their own text programs to their customers, such as rewards updates. Those programs will have their own sign-up screen, consent wording, and registration. They are not part of this program.
