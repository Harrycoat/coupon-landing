Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 25, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how Encore Marketing System ("we," "us," or "our") collects, uses, and protects information collected from merchants and their customers who interact with our SMS notification and marketing services (including the T-WORD demo experience).

Information We Collect

We collect mobile phone numbers and related contact information that merchants or end users voluntarily provide when signing up to receive text message notifications, including review requests, coupon alerts, and account updates.

How We Use Information

We use collected phone numbers solely to send the SMS notifications the user has opted in to receive, such as customer review reminders, coupon and promotional alerts, and service or account updates.

No Sharing of Mobile Information

We do not share, sell, or rent mobile phone numbers or SMS opt-in data with third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes. Mobile information is used only to deliver the messaging service described in this policy and is never shared with third parties for their own marketing purposes.

Message Frequency and Rates

Message frequency may vary depending on account activity. Message and data rates may apply.

Opt-Out

Users may opt out of receiving messages at any time by replying STOP to any message. Users may reply HELP for assistance. Users may opt back in at any time by replying START.

Data Security

We take reasonable measures to protect the information collected from unauthorized access, use, or disclosure.

Contact Us

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy, please contact us through the Encore Marketing System website.
