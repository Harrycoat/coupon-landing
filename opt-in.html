How Text Message Opt-In Works

Encore Marketing System, last updated October 4, 2026

Encore Marketing System provides loyalty, review-reply, and text message tools for small retail stores. This page shows exactly how a person opts in to receive text messages. Opting in is voluntary and is never required to buy anything.

Opt-in method 1: In-store sign-up tablet (QR or counter tablet)

At a participating store, the customer enters their own mobile number on a tablet at the counter. Before the number is saved, the customer sees the consent statements below and must check the box to join. The store records the date, time, and exact wording of each consent.

Join T mart Rewards

Mobile number: (___) ___-____
(Required to join the rewards program) I agree to receive recurring automated text messages from T mart (sent via Encore Marketing System) about my membership code, points, and reward coupons at the number provided. Message frequency varies. Message and data rates may apply. Reply STOP to cancel, HELP for help. Consent is not a condition of any purchase.
(Optional) I also agree to receive recurring automated promotional text messages (sales and special offers) from T mart (sent via Encore Marketing System). Message frequency varies. Message and data rates may apply. Reply STOP to cancel, HELP for help. Consent is not a condition of any purchase.

Join

Both boxes start unchecked. The promotional box is optional; a person can join the rewards program without agreeing to promotional messages. A person can still shop without joining.

Opt-in method 2: Website form (merchants)

On our website, merchants can request a consultation. The form has a separate, optional, unchecked box: "I agree to receive text messages from Encore Marketing System about my consultation request and service updates at the mobile number provided. Message frequency varies. Message and data rates may apply. Reply STOP to unsubscribe or HELP for help. Consent is not a condition of any purchase or of receiving a consultation." The page links to the Privacy Policy and Terms.

Confirmation, Help, and Opt-Out

Opt-in confirmation: T mart: Thanks for joining! Your member code is TW-1234. Msg frequency varies. Msg and data rates may apply. Reply HELP for help, STOP to cancel.
HELP reply: Encore Marketing System: For help email tmartbakersfield@gmail.com or call 661-373-7950. Msg and data rates may apply. Reply STOP to cancel.
STOP reply: Encore Marketing System: You have been unsubscribed and will receive no more messages. Reply START to rejoin.

Sample Messages

[T mart] Points added! You now have 3 of 5 visits. 2 more visits for a $5 coupon. Reply STOP to opt out.
[T mart] You earned a $5 coupon! Code CPN-123456. Show this text at checkout. Reply STOP to opt out.
[T mart] 20% off everything this weekend! Stop by and save. Reply STOP to opt out.

Promotional messages are sent only to people who checked the optional promotional box. Messages are sent only to people who opted in, and any number that replies STOP is removed from sending right away.
